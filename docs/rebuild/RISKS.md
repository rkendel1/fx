| R-21 | **Contract gaps not yet representable.** Some oracle behavior has no R1 representation: proposal identity annotations (`provisional_id`, `final_identity`, `argument_integrity`), the generic `*_KEY=` secret heuristic, and the 1,024-byte description truncation (provider shaping). | [BEHAVIOR_MATRIX.md](BEHAVIOR_MATRIX.md) §8.3. | M | M | Each is recorded as an explicit gap with its owning step. | R-22 | **The synchronous executor cannot interrupt a blocking capability call.** The core has no clock, thread or async runtime, so cancellation is observed only before `start`, between polls and through the host's `Flow::Stop`. | `rust/fx-core/src/executor.rs` module docs; ARCHITECTURE Primitive B. | M | M | The capability contract requires `poll` and `cancel` not to block indefinitely and makes the capability own the attempt budget (`Timeout`). R3 proves the Easy-LLM capability honors the budget; R4 adapters drive `poll` from the host's event loop. | R2-R4 |
| R-23 | **Intentional parity differences in the executor.** Provider records for undeclared tools fail closed at the executor (the oracle rejects at admission); `tool_calls` with nothing proposed is `InvalidCompletion`; streamed changed-id reconciliation is not implemented. | [BEHAVIOR_MATRIX.md](BEHAVIOR_MATRIX.md) §9.3-9.4. | M | L | Each difference fails closed and is recorded with its reason. Changed-id reconciliation stays a deferred gap until a capability is shown to need it (R3). | R2, R3 |
